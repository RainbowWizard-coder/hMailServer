Feather, Cole Bemis's icon set (https://feathericons.com), after whose drawings
the icons of hMailServer's webmail and the eye of its Control Deck are drawn.
Of the 56 symbols of the sprite of the webmail's page,
hmailserver/source/Server/Common/Util/Portal.html, eleven follow a Feather
icon's own drawing, some with their numbers rounded, some moved, resized or
drawn in about the centre: i-gear after "settings", i-pen after "edit-3",
i-print after "printer", i-inbox after "inbox", i-file after "file", i-draft
after "file-text", i-clip after "paperclip", i-signout after "log-in", i-popout
after "external-link", i-bell-off after "bell-off" and i-expand after
"maximize-2". i-close, i-plus and the four chevrons are Feather's "x", "plus"
and chevrons as they are. Others are near Feather's icon of the same subject,
as it is or drawn in about the centre by up to 15 per cent, as i-file is near
Feather's "file": among them i-search, i-star, i-folder, i-move, i-plane,
i-trash, i-warn, i-calendar, i-chat and i-storage.
build/generate-portal-page.py embeds the page in PortalPageData.cpp, which is
compiled into the server on Windows (hMailServer.exe) and Linux, and the server
serves it. The Control Deck's page, hmailserver/installation/WebAdmin/
index.html, which the server serves too, draws its eye after Feather's "eye",
laid into a box of 16 units; its other icons are plain circles and rectangles
with a few straight strokes. A simple shape - straight strokes on the grid's
whole units, with at most one plain circle or rectangle, such as three bars for
a menu or a box with a V for a letter - is drawn alike by line icons of one
subject on the 24-unit grid and is not counted as drawn after any icon set: so
i-menu, i-clock, i-mail and i-empty, which are point for point Tabler Icons'
"menu-2", "clock-hour-4" and "mail", and i-last, which is Lucide's
"chevron-last", are not credited to Tabler or to Lucide (Tabler Icons 3.49.0
and lucide-static 1.61.0, from the npm registry, were compared too). The
drawings were compared with those of
Feather's release 4.29.2, its dist/icons.json, from the npm registry
(feather-icons-4.29.2.tgz, whose sha512 equals the registry's integrity value,
sha512-0TaCFTnBTVCz6U+baY2UJNKne5ifGh7sMG4ZC2LoBWCZdIyPa+y6UiR4lEYGws1JOFWdee8KAsAIvu0VcXqiqA==).
