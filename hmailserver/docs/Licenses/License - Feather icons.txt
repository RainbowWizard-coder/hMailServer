Feather, Cole Bemis's icon set (https://feathericons.com), from whose drawings
eight of the icons of hMailServer's own pages are made. The webmail's page,
hmailserver/source/Server/Common/Util/Portal.html, draws seven of its icons
after Feather's, some with their numbers rounded, some moved or resized:
i-gear after "settings", i-pen after "edit-3", i-print after "printer", i-inbox
after "inbox", i-file after "file", i-draft after "file-text" and i-clip after
"paperclip". build/generate-portal-page.py embeds the page in PortalPageData.cpp,
which is compiled into the server on Windows (hMailServer.exe) and Linux, and the
server serves it. The Control Deck's page, hmailserver/installation/WebAdmin/
index.html, which the server serves too, draws its eye after Feather's "eye",
laid into a box of 16 units. The drawings were compared with those of
Feather's release 4.29.2, its dist/icons.json, from the npm registry
(feather-icons-4.29.2.tgz, whose sha512 equals the registry's integrity value,
sha512-0TaCFTnBTVCz6U+baY2UJNKne5ifGh7sMG4ZC2LoBWCZdIyPa+y6UiR4lEYGws1JOFWdee8KAsAIvu0VcXqiqA==).
