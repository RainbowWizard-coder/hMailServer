Feather, Cole Bemis's icon set (https://feathericons.com), after whose drawings
icons of hMailServer's webmail and the eye of its Control Deck are drawn.
Of the 56 symbols of the sprite of the webmail's page,
hmailserver/source/Server/Common/Util/Portal.html, eleven follow a Feather
icon's own drawing, some with their numbers rounded, some moved, resized or
drawn in about the centre: i-gear after "settings", i-pen after "edit-3",
i-print after "printer", i-inbox after "inbox", i-file after "file", i-draft
after "file-text", i-clip after "paperclip", i-signout after "log-in", i-popout
after "external-link", i-bell-off after "bell-off" and i-expand after
"maximize-2". i-close, i-plus and the four chevrons are Feather's "x", "plus"
and chevrons as they are. Others are near Feather's icon of the same subject,
as it is or drawn in about the centre by up to 15 per cent, as i-file is near
Feather's "file": among them i-search, i-star, i-folder, i-trash,
i-warn, i-calendar, i-chat and i-storage.
build/generate-portal-page.py embeds the page in PortalPageData.cpp, which is
compiled into the server on Windows (hMailServer.exe) and Linux, and the server
serves it. The Control Deck's page, hmailserver/installation/WebAdmin/
index.html, which the server serves too, draws its eye after Feather's "eye",
laid into a box of 16 units; its other icons are plain circles and rectangles
with a few straight strokes.
No other icon set is named. A set other than Feather would be named where a
symbol took from it more than simple shapes: plain figures - straight strokes,
a circle or a half circle, a box with square or rounded corners - laid out as
line icons of one subject on the 24-unit grid lay them, such as three bars for
a menu, a box with a V for a letter or a circle with a slash across it; an
outline drawn of curves, as a gear's, a pen's, a paperclip's or a bell's is, is
not one. That line decides only which set beyond Feather is named, not which
symbols are counted after Feather's, of which i-signout, i-popout and i-expand
are simple shapes. What the sprite has of other sets is simple shapes: i-menu,
i-clock, i-mail and i-empty are point for point Tabler Icons' "menu-2",
"clock-hour-4" and "mail"; i-archive has the lid, the box and the bar of
Tabler's "archive" where Tabler's has them, its lid square and its box's
corners of radius 1 where Tabler's are 2; i-junk is Humbleicons' "ban", its
circle and its stroke from 5.5 to 18.5; i-person has the half circle of
Lucide's "user-round" for its body and the head of Iconoir's "user";
i-mail-open is near Lucide's "mail-open", an icon Feather does not have;
i-last is Lucide's "chevron-last"; i-close, i-plus and the chevrons are
Lucide's and Tabler's as well as Feather's; and i-popout's box and diagonal
fall where Tabler's "external-link" has them, its strokes in Feather's order.
Tabler Icons 3.49.0, lucide-static 1.61.0 and 96 of Iconify's icon sets,
Humbleicons 1.29.0 and Iconoir 7.12.1 among them, each from the npm registry,
were compared too. The drawings were compared with those of
Feather's release 4.29.2, its dist/icons.json, from the npm registry
(feather-icons-4.29.2.tgz, whose sha512 equals the registry's integrity value,
sha512-0TaCFTnBTVCz6U+baY2UJNKne5ifGh7sMG4ZC2LoBWCZdIyPa+y6UiR4lEYGws1JOFWdee8KAsAIvu0VcXqiqA==).
